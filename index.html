Stage 1: Sensor Layer (Real-time Telemetry Acquisition)

Autonomous continuous sampling from low-latency sensors

I2C + ADC + GPIO

The safety pole's peripheral suite operates concurrently: the MPU-6050 samples tilt angles and collision jerk over I2C at 200ms intervals, the MQ-2 gas sensor streams analog voltage for combustible hydrocarbons, and the citizen SOS panic button sits on a noise-filtered interrupt.

About The Project

Unified Municipal Emergency Response

Traditional city surveillance systems operate in isolation: CCTV cameras record without alerting, gas leaks go unnoticed until ignited, and citizens in distress lack quick emergency contact points.

The Smart Street Safety Pole integrates existing low-cost safety technologies into one centralized platform. By leveraging edge microcontrollers and cloud synchronization, emergency response times are reduced from several minutes to under three seconds.

Scalable Deployment

Designed for existing street lighting poles without costly civil excavation.

Public Accountability

Every alert logs photographic evidence, exact GPS, and timestamped resolution.

Hardware Axis Verification

Accelerometer vector uses I2C registers 0x3B-0x3F. X-axis corresponds to lateral road pitch, and Y-axis corresponds to pole mast incline. Offsets persist across server restarts in calibration.json and Firebase RTDB.
